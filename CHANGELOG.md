# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-06)

First public release, with Hindi subtitles for three games:

- **The Last of Us Part I**: 46,084 lines. Pick हिन्दी under Options > Language > Subtitles.
- **Resident Evil 2**: 1,709 lines. Pick Polish under Options > Language > Display Language.
- **Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered**: 34,738 lines, including the DLC. Pick Danish under Settings > Text Language. Menus and voices stay in English.
